Proud Mary Coffee
Chocolate Bunnies
Limited Release · 50% Brazil Mantiqueira de Minas Natural · 50% Honduras Nahun Fernandez Natural · Medium Roast · Espresso Blend
Grind Size (Acaia Orbit SSP Lab Sweet V3) 1.6–1.7
Brew Temperature 202–204°F
Dose 18g
Yield 45–50g
Pressure 8 bar
Preinfusion 10s @ 2–3 bar
Total Shot Time 30–35s
Basket IMS 18g Precision High-Flow
Notes
Medium roast natural blend — far more soluble than your light roast coffees. Both components naturally processed, meaning heavier body, lower acidity, and rich chocolate/fruit sweetness. Expect little to no vegetal risk. Start coarser and lower temp vs. your Camber coffees. If it tastes flat or muddy, go slightly finer. If it tastes sharp or thin, lower temp slightly or coarsen. Designed to shine as espresso — should be syrupy and chocolatey straight through.
